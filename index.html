Questions owners ask

Do I need new software?

Usually a Zapier account plus the Gmail and Google Sheets you already have. If anything costs money, you hear the price before it’s set up.

Will an AI write to my customers?

Only in the way you approve. Most owners start with fixed replies and let the AI sort messages and flag the urgent ones.

Is it legal to text callers back?

Replying to someone who just called you is normal business texting, on a registered number. I don’t build mass texting or cold calling.

What happens if it breaks?

On monthly care I fix it. Without care I quote the fix before doing it.

Who runs OK Automation?

Jonah, a student in Chicago. I work with a few businesses at a time, so each setup gets my full attention.

Which businesses is it for?

Trades, shops, studios and salons that get calls, emails or web inquiries and can’t always answer. It isn’t for mass marketing.
