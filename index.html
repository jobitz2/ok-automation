The problem

Most small businesses don't lose customers on price. They lose them on follow-up.

When you're with a customer, on a ladder or closing up for the night, the phone rings and the inbox fills. Whoever answers first usually gets the job.

Missed calls go cold

A caller who hits voicemail usually tries the next business on the list instead of leaving a message.

Inquiries sit unread

Website messages and emails wait until evening, and by then the customer has booked someone else.

Leads live everywhere

Texts, emails, sticky notes. There's no single list of who reached out, what they need and who's waiting on you.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to buy new software?

Usually not. Most setups use what you already have, like Gmail, Google Sheets and your website form, plus an automation tool such as Zapier. Some tools have a small monthly cost, which you pay directly. I'll tell you before anything costs money.

Will an AI be talking to my customers?

Only if you want it to. By default the AI sorts messages and drafts replies, and you choose which kinds of replies go out automatically. Many owners start with fixed, pre-written replies and add AI later.

Is automated texting allowed?

Yes, when it's done right. Replying to someone who just called you is normal. Business texting in the US runs on a registered number, and I set that up properly. I don't build mass outbound texting.

What happens if something breaks?

With monthly care I monitor it and fix it, usually before you notice. Without it, you can still reach me and I'll quote any fix up front.

How long does a setup take?

A single automation usually takes about a week from our first call to running live, including testing it with you.

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Any small business that gets calls, emails or website inquiries and can't always respond right away: trades and home services, shops, studios, clinics, salons and more.
